Auto GI Process Integration

A cross-functional process connecting packing traceability, carton and pallet aggregation, QC/Production checkpoints, ERP Goods Issue, and Warehouse readiness.

RoleProcess Integration Contributor
ScopeProduction · QC · Warehouse · Automation · IT
Project TypePlant-Wide Process Design · ERP Integration
StatusPortfolio summary · high-level
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

The Goods Issue process depended on several upstream packing, traceability, quantity, quality, and warehouse-readiness conditions that were managed across separate operational steps.

T

Task

Support the design of an integrated future-state process that could verify readiness before ERP Goods Issue and handle exceptions clearly.

A

Action

Mapped the workflow from packing job start through Tool SN-to-box association, carton-to-pallet aggregation, packing-quantity reconciliation, Production and QC checkpoints, GI printing requirements, staging, and Warehouse handover. Defined process gates, ownership, expected system interactions, and exception scenarios.

R

Result

Produced a shared end-to-end process design that aligned operational teams and system requirements, creating a stronger foundation for controlled and automated Goods Issue execution.
