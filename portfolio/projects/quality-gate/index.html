Production Quality Gate, Buy-off & FAI Control

A connected workflow for Production and IPQC to control Buy-off, FAI, BOM validation, approvals, exceptions, and pending-job follow-up.

RoleProcess Designer & Solution Builder
ScopeProduction · IPQC · Warehouse · PMC
Project TypeFactory Applications · Quality Operations
StatusPortfolio summary · high-level
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

Quality-gate activities were distributed across separate operational steps, creating limited visibility into job status, approvals, exceptions, and whether Production could safely continue to the next stage.

T

Task

Define a consistent end-to-end process and build a digital control layer that connected Production and IPQC without adding unnecessary manual work.

A

Action

Mapped the Buy-off and FAI workflow, translated shop-floor rules into business logic, designed data structures and status models, built Power Apps interfaces, automated notifications and approvals, connected Databricks data, and created Power BI follow-up views. The solution also covered BOM comparison, rejection paths, pending-job control, and history tracking.

R

Result

Created one visible workflow for registration, confirmation, rejection, escalation, and follow-up. The system improved cross-functional coordination, made pending work easier to identify, and reduced reliance on fragmented manual tracking.
