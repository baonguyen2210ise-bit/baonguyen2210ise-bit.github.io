02 · Data Engineering · Production Traceability

Manufacturing Traceability & Data Audit

A job- and serial-level audit model that reconciles EOL, OneKey, Openlink, ERP, GI, and production records.

RoleData Analyst & Solution Designer
ScopeProduction · Quality · Warehouse · Data Systems
Project TypeData Engineering · Production Traceability
StatusPortfolio summary · high-level
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

Production records existed across multiple systems with different timestamps, identifiers, refresh cycles, and business meanings. This made it difficult to determine whether each unit had a valid trace and whether job-level quantities were consistent.

T

Task

Create a reliable audit layer that could reconcile records, expose missing or abnormal traces, and support investigation at both job and serial level.

A

Action

Standardized timestamps to Vietnam time, defined source priorities and matching rules, consolidated EOL, OneKey, Openlink, ERP, and GI data, and built SQL checks for missing traces, duplicate events, serial mismatches, abnormal model movement, incomplete jobs, and quantity discrepancies. Power BI views were added for summary and detailed tracing.

R

Result

Established a repeatable method for identifying trace gaps and production-data exceptions. Teams could move from a high-level discrepancy to the affected job, source, event, and serial more quickly.
