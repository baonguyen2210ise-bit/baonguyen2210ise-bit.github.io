08 · Undergraduate Thesis · Operations Research

Tank-Truck Transportation Route Optimization

A Python-based hybrid Vehicle Routing Problem model for LPG tank-truck scheduling, integrating multi-depot, multi-trip, split-delivery, capacity, cost, and driver working-time constraints.

Role Researcher & Optimization Model Developer
Study Scope 3 Depots · 24 Customers · Heterogeneous Fleet
Model Multi-Depot · Multi-Trip · Split-Delivery VRP
Technology Python · Savings · Genetic Algorithm · 2-opt
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

The existing LPG delivery approach typically assigned one customer per trip, which limited vehicle utilization and increased fixed and operating cost. In the baseline scenario, 27 vehicles were used with 66.48% average load efficiency and a 65.07% operating-time ratio.

T

Task

Develop a practical routing and scheduling model that could reduce total transportation cost while respecting real operating constraints such as vehicle capacity, multiple depots, multiple trips, split deliveries, customer service time, driver working hours, and mandatory rest periods.

A

Action

Formulated the problem as a multi-depot, multi-trip VRP with split delivery. Built a hybrid search in Python using a Savings-based initial solution, Genetic Algorithm operators, and 2-opt local search. The study used real operational demand, vehicle and cost parameters, HERE WeGo travel data, sensitivity analysis for GA settings, and OpenStreetMap visualization for the optimized routes.

R

Result

The best evaluated solution reduced total operating cost from 245,089,746 VND to 200,442,606 VND — a reduction of 44,647,140 VND (18.22%). Required vehicles fell from 27 to 16, load efficiency increased from 66.48% to 96.07%, and the operating-time ratio improved from 65.07% to 83.36%.
