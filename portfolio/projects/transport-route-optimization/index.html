08 · Undergraduate Thesis · Operations Research

Tank-Truck Transportation Route Optimization

A Python-based Vehicle Routing Problem model developed to improve tank-truck scheduling, reduce transportation cost, and support better fleet utilization.

RoleResearcher & Optimization Model Developer
OrganizationGas Shipping · Undergraduate Thesis
Project TypeVehicle Routing · Scheduling Optimization
TechnologyPython · Mathematical Modeling
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

Tank-truck transportation planning relied heavily on manual scheduling. The process had to account for delivery demand, vehicle capacity, routing constraints, and operational timing, creating opportunities for higher transport cost and uneven fleet utilization.

T

Task

Analyze the existing transportation problem and develop a scheduling approach that could assign routes and vehicles more efficiently while minimizing total transportation cost.

A

Action

Structured the operating requirements and constraints, formulated the problem as a Vehicle Routing Problem, implemented the optimization model in Python, and compared generated schedules with the existing manual-planning approach.

R

Result

The optimized schedules reduced total transportation cost by 6.47%–10.56% in the evaluated scenarios and improved overall fleet utilization compared with manual planning.
