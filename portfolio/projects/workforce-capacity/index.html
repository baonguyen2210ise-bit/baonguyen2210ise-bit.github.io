Workforce & Capacity Planning Tool

A planning model that connects production demand, required headcount, available manpower, hiring constraints, line capacity, and cross-zone transfer scenarios.

Role Process Designer & Planning Analyst
Scope Production · PMC · Management
Project Type Planning · Decision Support
Status Portfolio summary · high-level
STAR Case Study
S

Situation

Management needed to compare future demand with real production capacity and available manpower across several weeks, while also considering hiring limits and possible transfers between production zones.

T

Task

Build a planning tool that converted demand into capacity and manpower requirements and allowed decision-makers to test realistic resource scenarios.

A

Action

Structured multi-week demand inputs, modeled required and available headcount, incorporated hiring constraints and line capacities, and created cross-zone transfer scenarios. Outputs were organized to highlight shortages, capacity risks, and the effect of alternative staffing decisions.

R

Result

Provided one consistent basis for workforce and capacity discussions, helping management identify upcoming gaps earlier and evaluate transfer or hiring actions before they affected execution.
